Craft Suite

Six open-source, clean-room reimplementations of classic creative tools — pure Rust, compiled to WebAssembly, running entirely in your browser.

Drive these apps from Claude

A relay ships with the suite: Claude reads and edits your documents through the same tools you see here — four steps, about three minutes.

1

Get your two tokens

In the Railway dashboard, open the craft-relay service → Variables tab. You need:

  • MCP_TOKEN — proves it's Claude at the door
  • BRIDGE_TOKEN — proves it's your browser tab

Both are generated fresh when the template deploys — reveal and copy them.

2

Add the connector

In Claude Desktop: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then use:

Authorization: Bearer <MCP_TOKEN>

Paste your real MCP_TOKEN in place of the placeholder.

3

Connect a tab

Open any app above, click the ⦾ button (bottom-right corner), paste the BRIDGE_TOKEN — the relay is already wired, nothing else to fill in — and press Connect agent.

The page shows a persistent indicator while the bridge is live, and Disconnect is always one click away.

4

Try it

Ask Claude:

  • “What tabs are connected?”
  • “List the form fields in the PDF, then fill full_name and date_signed”
  • “Show me the PhotoCraft canvas”

Keep the app window visible while Claude works — hidden tabs pause their frame loop.

Nothing connects until you click Connect in the page; the bridge only ever drives the tab it lives in. A read-only toggle and Disconnect sit in the ⦾ panel. Works with 5 of the 6 apps today (all but VectorCraft).

Your work is saved in your browser (OPFS / IndexedDB), tied to this exact domain — no server-side storage, nothing leaves your device. Export anything you care about, and pick your final domain before you start. Chrome or Edge recommended (WebGPU).
